About my approach

I start with how customers actually behave.

I like taking complicated product information and turning it into something people actually understand, and trust enough to buy.

As a Computer Science graduate, I bring a research-driven, structural way of thinking to marketing problems. I look at why people hesitate before buying, what confuses them about a product, and what it takes to turn a curious browser into a confident customer.

I'm early in my career and looking for an entry-level Product Marketing Associate or Marketing Associate role, where I can apply this research-first approach to real products and real growth challenges.
